## Full description

Build words. Shape minds.

The official Sri Lanka Scrabble Academy app brings coaching, programmes, events and membership information together in one convenient place.

Use the app to:

- Explore beginner, school-development and competitive coaching programmes.
- Find Academy events and tournament information.
- Access tournament registration links.
- Apply for Sri Lanka Scrabble Academy membership.
- Contact the Academy for coaching, workshops and verified payment instructions.
- Follow official SLSA social media pages.
- Share the Academy with students, parents, teachers and Scrabble enthusiasts.

Sri Lanka Scrabble Academy develops vocabulary, strategic thinking and confidence through structured coaching and purposeful competition. Its programmes support beginners, school players, adults and competitive Scrabble players.

The app requires an internet connection for current website content. External registration, WhatsApp and social-media links open in the appropriate app or browser.

## Data safety draft

The native Android shell does not include advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

Optional registration and membership forms may ask for a name, phone number, age category, programme, preferred hub and WESPA rating. The website does not store these form entries. When the user submits a form, the app opens WhatsApp with a prepared message; the user decides whether to send it to SLSA.

For the Play Console Data safety form, review and declare at least:

- Personal information: name and phone number may be collected for registration, membership and support.
- Other optional information: age category, preferred hub and WESPA rating may be collected for app functionality.
- Data is sent over encrypted connections.
- Data collection is optional and user initiated.
- Data may be shared with WhatsApp/Meta when the user chooses to send a prepared message.
- The app does not create user accounts and therefore has no in-app account-deletion flow.
- External Google Forms and social-media services have their own privacy practices.
