Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Sri Lanka Scrabble Academy handles information when you use our website, Android app, registration tools and linked services.

Effective date: 16 September 2026

1. Who we are

Sri Lanka Scrabble Academy (“SLSA”, “we”, “our” or “us”) provides Scrabble coaching, school-development programmes, workshops, events and membership activities. This policy applies to the SLSA website and the Android app with package identifier lk.slsa.academy.

2. Information you may choose to provide

Registration and membership forms may ask for information such as:

  • Player or member name;
  • Phone number;
  • Age category and selected programme;
  • Preferred Academy hub; and
  • WESPA rating, when supplied voluntarily.

The website does not save these form entries in a website database. When you select a submit button, the website prepares a WhatsApp message containing the details you entered. You can review, edit or cancel that message before choosing whether to send it to SLSA.

3. How we use information

Information sent to SLSA may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries;
  • Process programme, event or membership requests;
  • Communicate relevant arrangements and verified payment instructions; and
  • Maintain records reasonably needed to administer Academy activities.

The website and app do not collect card or online-banking credentials. Payments are arranged separately using instructions confirmed directly by SLSA.

4. Children and junior players

SLSA works with junior Scrabble players. A parent, legal guardian or authorised school representative should complete registrations and communications for a child under 13. We do not knowingly ask a child under 13 to send personal information without appropriate adult involvement. If you believe that a child’s information was provided without proper authorisation, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

5. Website hosting and technical information

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The Android app securely loads the same website using an Android WebView. The app requires internet and network-state permissions but contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software development kits.

Hosting, internet and device providers may automatically process limited technical information, such as an IP address, browser or device type, request time and diagnostic logs, for security and service delivery under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. External services and links

The website and app contain links to services operated by other organisations, including WhatsApp, Google Forms, Facebook, YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. Selecting these links opens the relevant external app or website. Those services process information under their own privacy policies, and SLSA does not control their independent practices.

7. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only when you direct the app to an external service, when reasonably necessary to deliver an Academy activity, when working with a school or event organiser authorised for that activity, or when disclosure is required by law.

8. Retention and deletion

Messages and registration records received by SLSA are kept only for as long as reasonably needed for the relevant enquiry, programme, event, membership, safeguarding, accounting or legal purpose. You may ask us to correct or delete information you previously sent. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate record-keeping.

9. Security

The app loads SLSA content over HTTPS and refuses invalid secure connections. Although we use reasonable safeguards, no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website, app, services or legal requirements change. The current version and effective date will remain available on this page.
