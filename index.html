About SLSA

A place for curious minds and competitive players.

Sri Lanka Scrabble Academy develops vocabulary, strategic thinking and confidence through structured coaching and purposeful competition.

Led by experienced coach and administrator Raashid Sudin, it consists of high-calibre individuals with expertise and experience, including Imeth De Silva, Kavindu Mallawaraarachchi and Zainab Sultan. The academy supports young players, schools and adults at every stage of the game.
